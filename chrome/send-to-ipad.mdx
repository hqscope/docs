If you mark up readings and slides on an iPad, Scope can send them there for you. Click **Send to Lectra** on a PDF in Chrome, and it arrives in Lectra, ready for Apple Pencil. When you're done, you can attach the marked-up file to a Canvas assignment or Gradescope without downloading anything.

Lectra is Scope's free iPad app for reading and annotating PDFs. To learn about it, see the [Lectra overview](/lectra/overview).

## Before you begin

- Install Lectra on your iPad and sign in. See [Install Lectra](/lectra/install).
- Open Lectra on your iPad at least once after you sign in.
- Sign in to Scope in Chrome with the same Google account you use in Lectra. See [Sign in to Scope](/chrome/sign-in).
- Turn on **Send to Lectra** in Scope, as described in the following section. It's off until you turn it on.

## Turn on Send to Lectra

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the Scope icon, then click the **Settings** icon (the gear).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Under **Features**, turn on **Send to Lectra**. Scope confirms **On. You'll see Send to Lectra on PDFs you open.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

After you sign in, the Scope popup may also offer **Have an iPad? Turn on Send to Lectra to mark up PDFs with Apple Pencil.** Click **Turn On** to do the same thing.

While **Send to Lectra** is on, the bottom of the popup shows whether Lectra is ready: **Lectra connected**, **Lectra not connected**, or **Sign in to send to Lectra**.

### Allow PDFs saved on your computer

To send PDFs that you open from your own computer (not from a website), Chrome needs one more permission. If Scope shows **PDFs saved on this computer need one more permission.**, click **How to allow local PDFs**, then turn on **Allow access to file URLs** on the page that opens.

## Send a PDF

Where you start depends on where the file is.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="A PDF in Canvas">
    Open the PDF in Canvas. A round **Send to Lectra** button appears in the corner of the page. Click it.

    To move the button out of the way, press and hold it, then drag. Scope remembers where you put it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A PDF on any website">
    Open the PDF in Chrome. A **Send to Lectra** button appears on the PDF. Click it.

    This also works for Google Docs, Slides, and Sheets, which Scope sends as PDFs, and for PDFs in Google Drive.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A link to a file">
    Right-click a link to a PDF or other document, then choose **Send to Lectra**. Chrome shows a notification while it sends and when it's done.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From the Scope popup">
    With the PDF open in the current tab, click the Scope icon, click **More actions** (the three dots), and choose **Send to Lectra**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="A file on your computer">
    Drag a file from your computer onto the open Scope popup. When you see **Drop a file to send to Lectra**, let go.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

When you use a **Send to Lectra** button or the popup's **More actions** menu, Scope asks you to confirm with the file name. Click **Send**. Sends from the right-click menu and from dragging a file start right away.

## What you can send

- PDFs, from any of the places in the previous section.
- Slides, documents, spreadsheets, and images, when you drag them onto the popup or right-click a link to them: PowerPoint, Keynote, Word, Pages, Excel, Numbers, rich text, plain text, CSV, PNG, JPEG, HEIC, GIF, WebP, and TIFF files. Lectra turns them into PDFs on your iPad.
- Files up to 50 MB each.

## After you send

The file goes to **Scope Inbox** in Lectra. If it came from a Canvas course, Lectra files it in a folder named after the course. To find it on your iPad, see [Bring documents into Lectra](/lectra/import).

Scope tells you what happened:

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Sent to Lectra. It'll show up in your Scope Inbox in a moment.** | The file is on its way to your iPad. |
| **Sent. It'll appear the next time you open Lectra.** | Your iPad couldn't be reached right now. The file waits for you. |
| **Open Lectra on your iPad once, then send again.** | Lectra hasn't been opened on an iPad with your account yet. Open it, then send again. |
| **Updated in Lectra.** | You replaced a copy that was already in Lectra. |

### If the PDF is already in Lectra

If you send a PDF that's already in your Lectra library, Scope shows **Already in Lectra** and lets you choose:

- **Send a Copy**: add a second copy, named with "(copy)" at the end.
- **Replace in Lectra**: swap the PDF in Lectra for this one. Your notes on it stay with the document.
- **Cancel**: send nothing.

## Send a whole course

You can send every PDF in a Canvas course's **Files** to Lectra in one step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the course's Files page">
    In Canvas, go to the course and click **Files**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the course menu">
    Click the Scope icon, click **More actions** (the three dots), and choose **Import course to Lectra**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose folders">
    Scope shows the course's folders and how many PDFs are in each. Clear any folder you don't want. The bottom of the window shows how many PDFs are selected and how many are already in Lectra and will be updated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click **Import to Lectra**. Scope shows its progress, then how many PDFs it imported and updated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Lectra">
    Click **Open Lectra**, or open Lectra on your iPad. Your course is in your library.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Import course to Lectra** appears only on a Canvas course's **Files** page, and only while **Send to Lectra** is on. It sends PDFs only.
</Note>

## Save a whole course to your computer

You can also download every PDF in a Canvas course to your computer. You don't need to sign in or turn on **Send to Lectra** for this.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the course's Files page">
    In Canvas, go to the course and click **Files**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the download">
    Click the Scope icon, click **More actions**, and choose **Download course to computer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose folders and save">
    In **Download course**, clear any folder you don't want, then click **Download course**. The Scope icon in your toolbar shows how many files are left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the folder">
    When it's done, Scope shows how many PDFs it saved. Click **Show folder** to see them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Scope saves the PDFs in your **Downloads** folder, in a folder named after the course, with the course's subfolders inside it. Downloading the same course again replaces the earlier copies.

## Attach your marked-up work to an assignment

When you've finished a document in Lectra, you can attach it to a Canvas assignment or a Gradescope submission from Chrome.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the upload form">
    In Canvas, open the assignment and start a file upload. In Gradescope, open the submission window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select from Lectra">
    Click **Select from Lectra**, next to Canvas's file upload or Gradescope's **Select PDF** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the document">
    In the list, find the document. Documents you've written on show **Annotated**. You can search with **Search your Lectra documents…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Scope attach it">
    Scope shows **Getting the latest from your iPad…**, then **Attaching…**, then that the document is ready. The file is in the upload form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Check the file and submit the assignment yourself. Scope never submits for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your iPad doesn't answer in time, Scope offers to attach the most recent backed-up copy instead, or to try again. For the latest marks to be included, keep Lectra open on your iPad while you attach.

<Note>
  **Select from Lectra** appears only while **Send to Lectra** is on and you're signed in. In Canvas, it appears on assignments that accept file uploads.
</Note>

## Receive files from Lectra

When you send a document from Lectra to your computer, Scope saves it to your **Downloads** folder. This works while **Send to Lectra** is on and you're signed in to Scope. To see the status, open **Settings** > **Features** and look under **Send to Lectra**. **Connected** with **Ready to receive files** means Scope is ready. While a file arrives, it shows **Downloading Lectra file** and the file name.

To learn how to send from your iPad, see [Send files between devices](/lectra/send-between-devices).

If you also use the Lectra Receiver extension for Chrome, Scope hides its own **Send to Lectra** button on website PDFs and its **Select from Lectra** buttons, so you don't see two of each. See [Lectra Receiver](/lectra/receiver).

## Check your Lectra storage

Files you send to Lectra use your Lectra storage. When you're signed in, the top of **Settings** shows how much you've used, such as **Lectra storage: 1.2 GB of 5 GB used**. To manage it, open Lectra on your iPad.

## If a send doesn't work

If a send fails, Scope shows **Couldn't send to Lectra** with the reason.

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Sign in to Scope, then send again.** | Sign in to Scope. |
| **Turn on Send to Lectra in Scope's settings, then send again.** | Turn on **Send to Lectra**. |
| **No PDF found on this page. Open the PDF on its own, then send again.** | Open the PDF by itself in a tab, then send. |
| **This page only shows the PDF. Open the PDF itself, then send again.** | The page shows the PDF inside a viewer. Open or download the PDF itself. |
| **This doesn't look like a PDF. Open the PDF on its own page, then send again.** | You may have been signed out of the site. Sign in and open the PDF again. |
| **Scope can't open this PDF from here. Open it on its own, then send again.** | Open the PDF in its own tab. For Google Drive, make sure you're using the Google account that can open the file. |
| **This PDF is over 50 MB, the most you can send to Lectra.** | Send a smaller file, or split it. |
| **This PDF is empty, so there's nothing to send.** | Check the file. |
| **This PDF couldn't be read. Try another copy of it.** | Download a fresh copy and send that. |
| **This PDF is already on its way to Lectra.** | Wait for the first send to finish. |
| **You're offline. Your PDF is still here. Try again when you're connected.** | Reconnect, then click **Try Again**. |
| **Something didn't go through. Nothing was changed in Lectra. Try again in a moment.** | Click **Try Again**. |
| Your Lectra storage is full | Free up space in Lectra on your iPad, then send again. |

For more fixes, see [Troubleshooting](/chrome/troubleshooting#send-to-lectra).
