Scope is built to work from the keyboard. You can open the search bar, move through results, ask a question, and pin an item without touching the mouse.

On a Mac, use <kbd>⌘</kbd> (Command). On Windows, ChromeOS, and Linux, use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>.

## Open and close the search bar

These keys work on Canvas and Brightspace pages.

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> | Open the search bar. |
| <kbd>/</kbd> | Open the search bar. Doesn't work while you're typing in a text field on the page. |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close the search bar. If an answer is showing, the first <kbd>Esc</kbd> goes back to your results and the second closes the search bar. |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Move between the search box, the links and buttons in an answer, and the follow-up box. Focus stays inside the search bar until you close it. |

<Note>
  If the shortcuts don't respond right after a page loads, click anywhere on the page once and try again. On pages that aren't Canvas or Brightspace, click the Scope icon in your toolbar instead.
</Note>

### Turn off the / shortcut

If <kbd>/</kbd> gets in your way, open the Scope popup, go to **Settings** > **Features**, and turn off **Open search with /**. <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> keep working.

## Move through results

These keys work in the search bar and in the toolbar popup.

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>↑</kbd> | Highlight the next or previous result. |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open the highlighted result in the current tab. On an **Ask Scope** row, ask the question. On a command row, run the command. |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> | Pin or unpin the highlighted result. |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + click | Open a result in a new background tab. |

## Ask questions and follow up

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | In **Ask a follow-up…**, send your follow-up. |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> | In **Ask a follow-up…**, start a new line. |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Leave the answer and go back to your search results. |

## In the toolbar popup

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close **Settings**, **Help & Shortcuts**, the **More actions** menu, or another open panel. |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Move between the search box, tabs, and buttons. |

To see a short list of shortcuts inside Scope, open the popup and go to **Settings** > **Help & shortcuts**.

## Change the shortcut

Scope's shortcuts are built into its course-page search bar, so you can't change them in Chrome's keyboard shortcut settings. You can only turn off the <kbd>/</kbd> shortcut, as described earlier.
