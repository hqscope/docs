Besides searching, the Scope search bar on Canvas and Brightspace pages runs commands. You type a command's name, and its row appears at the top of the results. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to run it.

## Run a command

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the search bar">
    On a Canvas or Brightspace page, press <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, or press <kbd>/</kbd>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the command">
    Type the command's name, such as `gpa`. To see every command, type `/` in the search box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    Highlight the command's row and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or click it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A command's row shows its name, a short description, and a label for what it does, such as **OPEN**, **ADD**, or **AI**. Commands match only their exact name, or the name with one typo, so ordinary searches aren't taken over by commands.

Some commands take text after the name, such as `note buy lab goggles`. If you choose one of these without text, Scope puts the command name in the box and waits for you to type the rest.

<Note>
  Commands run only in the search bar on course pages. They aren't in the toolbar popup.
</Note>

## Study and AI commands

| Command | What to type | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Ask Scope** | Any question, such as `when is the midterm?` | Answers questions about your courses, and can add study to-dos and calendar blocks. See [Ask Scope](/chrome/ask-scope). |
| **Ask Scope** (quick answer) | `ask` and a question, such as `ask what's the late policy` | A short answer from your course materials, with numbered sources. |
| **Smart study plan** | `plan`, `planner`, or `studyplan` | A study plan for the next two weeks from your deadlines. See [Make a study plan](/chrome/study-plan). |
| **Practice quiz** | `quiz` or `practice` | A short quiz with an answer key from your course materials. See [Practice quizzes and exams](/chrome/practice). |
| **Practice exam** | `/exam` and a course, such as `/exam chem 3a` | A full practice exam with an answer key, from one course. The slash is required. |
| **Daily briefing** | `briefing`, `brief`, or `today` | What's due soon, where you stand on grades, and what to focus on today. |
| **Syllabus Autopilot** | `syllabus`, `autopilot`, or `scheduler` | Reads the syllabus on the page and adds its deadlines to your agenda and Google Calendar. See [Add syllabus deadlines to your calendar](/chrome/syllabus-deadlines). |
| **Study in Polya** | `polya` | Sends the Canvas course you're looking at to Polya. See [Send a course to Polya](/chrome/study-in-polya). |

## Course tools

| Command | What to type | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **GPA calculator** | `gpa`, or `gpa` and a saved scenario's name | Opens the GPA calculator with your current grades. |
| **Grades summary** | `grades` or `grade` | Shows your current grade in every Canvas course on one screen. |
| **Focus timer** | `timer`, `focus`, `pomodoro`, `zen`, or `study` | Opens Focus Mode with a work and break timer and background sound. |
| **Quick note** | `note` and your note | Saves a note. |
| **Browse notes** | `notes` | Shows all your notes. |
| **Add to-do** | `todo` or `task`, and the to-do | Adds a to-do to your agenda. |
| **Set reminder** | `remind` and what and when, such as `remind essay in 2h` | Sets a one-time reminder notification. Needs **Deadline reminders** on in Settings. |

For details on each tool, see [Grades, notes, and focus tools](/chrome/course-tools) and [Track what's due](/chrome/due-dates).
