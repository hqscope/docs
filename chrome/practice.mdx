Scope can write practice questions from your own course materials, so you study what your course actually covered. Use a **Practice quiz** for a short check, or a **Practice exam** for a full test from one course with an answer key and sources for every question.

## Before you begin

- Let Scope read your courses first. The more course pages and files Scope has read, the better the questions. See [Connect your courses](/chrome/connect-your-courses).
- Sign in to Scope to build practice exams. See [Sign in to Scope](/chrome/sign-in).

## Take a practice quiz

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the search bar">
    On a Canvas or Brightspace page, press <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type quiz">
    Type `quiz` or `practice`. The **Practice quiz** row appears with an **AI** label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Scope writes a short quiz from your course materials, with the answer key after the questions and numbered sources you can click.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To ask for a different focus, type a follow-up in **Ask a follow-up…**, such as "make it about chapter 5".

## Build a practice exam

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the search bar">
    On a Canvas or Brightspace page, press <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type /exam and the course">
    Type `/exam` followed by the course, for example `/exam bio 1a`. The **Practice exam** row appears. You need the slash: typing `exam` alone searches your courses instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build it">
    Press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Scope shows **Build a practice exam for** and the course name, then writes the exam.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you leave out the course, Scope uses the course you're looking at. If it can't tell, it asks **Which course is the exam for?** and shows a button for each course. Click one.

### What a practice exam looks like

- **Practice Exam**: about 10 questions, a mix of multiple choice (A to D) and short answer, from easier recall questions to harder applied ones.
- **Answer Key**: after a line, with the correct answer and a short explanation for each question.
- Numbered sources after each question and answer, which link to the course material it came from.

<Tip>
  The answer key is visible right away. To test yourself, scroll only as far as the questions, write your answers, and then check the key.
</Tip>

### Reopen or rebuild an exam

Scope saves the last exam you built for each course on this browser. When you run `/exam` for that course again, it opens the saved exam, labeled **Saved exam** and the date.

- To build a new one, click **Build a Fresh Exam**.
- To replace the exam you're looking at, click **Regenerate**.

## Messages you might see

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Sign in to build practice exams.** | Sign in to Scope, then try again. You can still open an exam you saved earlier. |
| **Scope hasn't read any of your courses yet. Open a few course pages or files, then try again.** | Open your course site and let Scope finish finding your courses. |
| **No course matches** and the name you typed | Pick the course from the list Scope shows. |
| **No exam came back. Try again in a moment.** | Try again. |
| **Couldn't build the exam. Try again in a moment.** | Try again. If it keeps happening, the course may not have enough material that Scope can read yet. |

<Note>
  Practice questions are written by AI from your course materials. Check anything that looks wrong against the source it cites, and ask your instructor when in doubt.
</Note>
