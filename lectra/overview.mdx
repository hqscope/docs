description: "Lectra is a free iPad app for reading and marking up PDFs with Apple Pencil, keeping handwritten notebooks, scanning paper, and running Python, all in one place."
sidebarTitle: "Overview"
---

Lectra is a free app for iPad where you read, mark up, and work on your course documents. You open a PDF, write on it with Apple Pencil, add typed notes, and keep blank notebook pages and scans next to it. When the reading turns into work, Python notebooks, a terminal, and a code editor live in the same app.

Your library lives on your iPad and works without an internet connection. You don't need an account to use Lectra. If you sign in, Lectra also keeps a backup copy of your pages.

<Info>
  On the App Store, Lectra is listed as **Lectra Notes**. It is free, with no ads and no tracking.
</Info>

## What you need

- An iPad with iPadOS 18 or later. For the full list, see [System requirements](/get-started/system-requirements).
- Apple Pencil is recommended for handwriting.
- An account is optional. You need one only to back up your pages and to receive files sent from Scope. To learn more, see [Your Scope account](/get-started/account).
