Scope builds tools that make you more capable and independent. They handle everything around learning, like finding the reading, keeping your notes in order, and checking your understanding, so you can do the learning yourself.

All Scope apps are free. They work alongside the Canvas or Brightspace course your school already uses. New here? Start with [What is Scope?](/get-started/overview) or go straight to the [quickstart](/get-started/quickstart).

## The apps

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Scope extension" icon="chrome" href="/chrome/overview">
    Search everything in your Canvas and Brightspace courses from one search bar in Chrome, ask questions about your course, and send PDFs to your iPad.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lectra" icon="pen-nib" href="/lectra/overview">
    Read and mark up PDFs with Apple Pencil, take handwritten notes, record lectures, and run Python on your iPad.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Polya" icon="graduation-cap" href="/polya/overview">
    Work through problems with a tutor that answers from your own course materials and shows where each answer came from.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Not sure which one you need? See [Choose the right app](/get-started/choose-an-app).
