## Ask Scope or Polya?

Both answer questions from your course materials. They suit different moments.

| | Ask Scope | Polya |
|---|---|---|
| Where it is | The search bar in Chrome, on your Canvas pages | askpolya.com, in any web browser, including on your phone |
| Best for | Quick facts about your course: dates, policies, what's due, where a file is | Working through concepts and problems until you understand them |
| How much it gives away | Answers questions about your course. It isn't set up to do graded work for you | Follows the study mode you choose for each course. By default, it starts with a hint |
| Needs | The Scope extension, signed in | A Scope account and a course added to Polya |

## If you use more than one app

The apps share one Scope account. To move files between them, sign in to each app with the same Google account. To learn more, see [Your Scope account](/get-started/account).

<Tip>
  To set up all three apps in about 10 minutes, follow the [quickstart](/get-started/quickstart).
</Tip>
